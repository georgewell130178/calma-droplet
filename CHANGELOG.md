# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-23

- Ambient sound, synthesised live rather than looped: waves, rain, fire and brown noise.
- Guided breathing with three patterns — Balance 5-5, Box 4-4-4-4 and Relax 4-7-8 — for 1, 3, 5 or 10 minutes, rounded up so a session ends on a whole breath.
- The sound follows the breath: the waves roll in on the inhale and draw back on the exhale, and the other sounds swell and soften.
- Shelf widget in both layouts, with the orb, the sounds, the volume and the session.
- A panel in the notch and a menu bar extra, so Calma works without the shelf open.
- A live activity beside the notch while a sound or a session runs, and a "Session complete" HUD with your streak.
